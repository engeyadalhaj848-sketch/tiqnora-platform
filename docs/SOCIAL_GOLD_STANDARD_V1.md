# Tiqnora Social Gold Standard v1

Status: production quality gate. Automatic publishing stays disabled until approved visual assets are configured.

## Purpose

Every public post must make Tiqnora look like a premium Saudi technology company, not an AI template generator. A post is publishable only when copy, visual and channel adaptation all pass review.

## Non-negotiable visual rules

- Brand palette: Midnight Navy #060B1E, Electric Blue #0A5CFF, Cyber Cyan #00D2FF, Pure White #FFFFFF.
- Use the approved Tiqnora logo only.
- One focal idea per visual.
- Mobile-first typography: large headline, strong contrast, generous spacing.
- Prefer premium realistic business scenes and polished service demonstrations.
- No wireframes, placeholder rectangles, generic dashboard grids, fake UI, random product photos, stock-market imagery, crowded collages, tiny text, cartoon robots, neon brains or recycled legacy templates.
- A visual asset must be explicitly approved and present in `organization.settings.social_autopilot.approved_assets` before autopilot can queue it.

## Copy rules

- One customer pain + one value proposition + one CTA.
- Natural Saudi Arabic; professional and human.
- No fixed service prices in organic service marketing.
- No fabricated statistics, customer names, testimonials, rankings, guarantees or ROI.
- Avoid generic filler such as “حلول متكاملة”, “نقلة نوعية”, “ثورة رقمية” and “أفضل الحلول” unless concrete evidence makes the phrase meaningful.
- Facebook, Instagram and TikTok copy must be adapted to the channel rather than copied verbatim.
- Maximum five useful hashtags by default.

## Review flow

Chief of Staff → Marketing → Content/Social → Visual specialist → Chief of Staff final review.

The final reviewer must reject and return work to one named specialist when any element is weak. Automatic organic publishing is allowed only after the final review and an explicitly approved Gold Standard visual. Paid ads and direct customer outreach remain outside this authority.

## Gold Standard starter set

1. Web design / e-commerce — clarity, speed, trust and conversion path.
2. WhatsApp automation — organized inquiry handling, CRM handoff and human escalation.
3. AI agents — automate repetitive coordination while keeping human review at decision points.

These three items are stored as draft content records with `source=gold_standard_v1` and remain publish-blocked until their visuals are approved.

- Immediate owner-review batches are one-time armed and still remain Telegram approval-gated before publish.
